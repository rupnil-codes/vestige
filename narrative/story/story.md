
> Pacing goal: ~15 mins

The progression would be like:
1. Game opens with the protagonist sitting in a bench at the middle of the woods.
2. Very foggy atmosphere with natural ambience. Lightly snowing.
3. "E" to stand up.
4. One Silhouette spawns, standing behind a tree almost like calling you.
5. If you choose to "touch it" it vanishes and leads you to the first Vestige.
   - Vestige: Refers to a forgotten remnant or memory i.e., A swing, Piano, etc., Makes you feel nostalgic
6. As soon as you reach each vestige (tbd) a set sequence plays that feels too close to home, yet you don't remember.
7. Finally, after visiting a set number of Vestiges, the silhouette leads you to the Watch Tower.
8. A bunker door with a heavy lock is visible.
9. If you follow the path and skip the Vestiges the Watch Tower would have all furniture but no silhouette or sounds.
10. You climb the tall stairs of the Watch Tower.
11. At the top you find a single room, with only a table-chair-desklamp and the silhouette curled up in a corner.
12. As you enter the room and move towards, it vanishes again.
13. You experience the last vestige and make your way down.
14. The bunker door lays wide open.
15. You move down the stairs -- the door closes behind you (silently with a click)
16. A long tunnel stretches your vision.
17. As you move through the impossibly long corridor you hear and see traces of the previous vestiges -- pictures, sounds, etc in reverse order.
18. No silhouette guides you, you are all alone with electric hums and occasional vestiges.
19. Finally, you spot the finishing stairs, you climb up.
20. The same bench from the first scene lays in front of you -- facing your direction!
21. Let the player explore that the can sit -- Press "E" to sit only when they get close enough. 
22. As soon as the player moves out of the tunnel, it disappears.
23. If the player chooses to sit the same animation in reverse takes place and game ends.
24. Else the world is exactly the same -- without the paths and the watch tower.
25. The player can stall as much as they want, but eventually they have to sit down.
26. Ending the game -- rolling credits.
